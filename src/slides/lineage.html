Sources: papers and release posts

PFNs in 2021 introduced training a network on synthetic datasets to do Bayesian prediction. TabPFN v1 in 2022 handled small classification tasks. 2024 added TabForestPFN and Layer6's TabDPT, which trains on real data. 2025 had TabPFN v2 in Nature, TabICL from INRIA, Amazon's Mitra, SAP's ContextTab, StableAI's LimiX and TabPFN-2.5. TabICLv2 came out in February 2026, fully open source. Then Kumo's relational KumoRFM-2 in April, TabPFN-3 in May, Google's TabFM in June, and in September TabPFN-3.5, LimiX-2, Amazon's Mitra-v2 and NVIDIA's Kumo Tabular.
