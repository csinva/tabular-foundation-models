ELO VS INFERENCE TIME (NVIDIA)

Up and left is better. Kumo Tabular L matches LimiX-2 at ~17× less time per sample.

Bars: one table in the TabPFN-3.5 paper (arXiv 2609.17895), start at 1300. *Own runs: LimiX-2 (16 Sep), Kumo Tabular (29 Sep). Figure: NVIDIA HF blog.

Left: TabArena Elo. The solid bars all come from one table in the TabPFN-3.5 paper, so they are comparable with each other; the table is from Prior Labs. The two hatched bars with asterisks are self-reported by their authors from separate runs: LimiX-2 at 1935 and NVIDIA Kumo Tabular at 1950. Right: NVIDIA's own figure of Elo against median inference time per thousand samples, log scale, on one RTX 6000 Pro. Up and to the left is better. Kumo Tabular's three sizes form the frontier; LimiX-2 reaches similar Elo but is about 17 times slower. Tuned LightGBM sits near 1,400.
