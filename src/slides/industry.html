The companies building tabular foundation models. Two of the startups were bought this year: Prior Labs by SAP, with a commitment of over a billion euros, and Kumo by NVIDIA, terms undisclosed with about 400 million dollars reported. Fundamental raised 255 million dollars for a closed model with no public benchmarks. Google released TabFM in June and serves it in BigQuery. H2O.ai launched tabH2O in May as an API, built on the TabICL architecture. Amazon released Mitra-v2 in September under Apache 2.0. SAP also has its own RPT-1 product. StableAI released LimiX-2 with Tsinghua. Neuralk-AI in Paris has a proprietary model and the TabBench benchmark. Layer6, TD Bank's lab, made TabDPT, trained on real data. Lexsi Labs released the Orion models in 2025. Microsoft has research models like MotherNet and TabFlex.
