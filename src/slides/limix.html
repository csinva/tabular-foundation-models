LimiX from StableAI and Tsinghua is the clearest example of a tabular foundation model doing more than classification and regression. Instead of learning only p of y given x, it learns the joint distribution of all cells given the context, by masking cells during pretraining and reconstructing them. At inference the mask defines the task. Mask the label of the query rows and it is a classifier or regressor. Mask missing feature cells and it imputes. The first LimiX paper also uses it to generate data. LimiX-2 adds causal structure: make each variable the target in turn and look at which features its attention concentrates on; thresholding those scores gives an undirected causal skeleton. Across six standard causal datasets this reaches a mean F1 of 0.797, against 0.659 for the next best model, EXAONE Tabular. It still scores 1935 Elo on TabArena by its own report. Imputation and anomaly scoring are described but not benchmarked in the LimiX-2 paper.
