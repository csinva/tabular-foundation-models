NVIDIA released Kumo Tabular in three sizes, and the interesting part is how they line up against everything else. These values are read off NVIDIA's own chart of TabArena Elo against median inference time per thousand rows, so treat them as approximate and self-reported. The small model, 28 million parameters, the same size as TabICLv2, scores about 1,795 Elo, level with TabPFN-3.5-Fast and well above TabICLv2, and it is the fastest of these. The large model, 215 million parameters, tops the chart at around 1,950 to 1,960, level with LimiX-2, but it takes about half a second per thousand rows where LimiX-2 takes about nine, roughly 19 times slower; NVIDIA quotes 17 times. It is also slightly ahead of TabPFN-3.5 at the same speed and a similar parameter count. The medium model's size is not published. All three use the same compress-rows design as TabICLv2, and the weights are under OpenMDW, which allows commercial use.
