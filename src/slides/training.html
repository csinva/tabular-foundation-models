Training numbers. About 2 million steps with 64 tables each, so roughly 130 million tables. One node with eight RTX 2080 Ti GPUs for about two weeks. The default model has 12 layers, embedding size 192, 6 heads. The paper does not state a parameter count; about 7 million is my calculation from the released config. Loss is cross-entropy on held-out rows. Classification and regression are separate checkpoints. For regression the output is a distribution over 5,000 bins whose edges are quantiles of targets sampled from the prior, trained with cross-entropy over bins. This gives a full predictive distribution, not only a point estimate.
